## Summary
Cart latency rose fivefold and some customers saw empty carts for 50 minutes.

## Timeline
- 09:40 session-cache primary failed over; `cache_hit_ratio` dropped from 0.97 to 0.3.
- 09:45 cart WARN logs `session cache miss ... rebuilding`.
- 10:30 cache warm; latency normal.

## Root cause
The replica had replication disabled during a maintenance window three days earlier and was never re-enabled, so it was promoted cold.

## Remediation
- Replication health now alerts; runbook RB-012 updated.

## Lessons
- Cache-miss storms have two shapes: eviction (configuration) and cold start (failover). Evictions above zero point at configuration.
