## Summary
`order.placed` lag reached 1,200 seconds during a fulfillment worker rollout.

## Timeline
- 11:00 rollout started with maxUnavailable=5 on six replicas.
- 11:08 `fulfillment-consumer-lag` fired.
- 11:30 rollout complete; backlog drained by 11:50.

## Root cause
Rollout strategy allowed five of six consumers to be unavailable at once.

## Remediation
- maxUnavailable set to 1 for all consumers.

## Lessons
- A backlog with healthy consumers is a capacity question: how many consumers exist right now?
