## Summary
Customer emails were delayed by up to two hours during a Mailrelay API outage.

## Timeline
- 13:15 notifications error rate rose to 100% with HTTP 503 responses from api.mailrelay.internal.
- 13:20 Mailrelay's status feed reported a major outage in all regions.
- 15:05 vendor recovered; the backlog drained in 25 minutes.

## Root cause
Vendor-side outage. DNS resolution of the Mailrelay endpoint worked throughout; the responses were vendor 503s, not connection failures.

## Remediation
- Added the status feed to the notifications dashboard.

## Lessons
- Distinguish cannot-resolve-or-connect from vendor-returns-errors; only the latter is the vendor's outage.
