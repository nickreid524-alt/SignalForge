## Summary
The nightly reports failed when the reporting node ran out of disk.

## Timeline
- 2025-06-21 new ETL stage introduced.
- 2025-06-30 02:10 job failed with `No space left on device`.
- 09:00 temp files removed; job rerun.

## Root cause
The stage wrote per-run extracts to `/var/tmp/reporting` and relied on a cleanup step that was never enabled.

## Remediation
- Cleanup enabled; `disk-usage-warning` threshold lowered to 75%.

## Lessons
- Disk problems on batch nodes are almost always retention problems.
