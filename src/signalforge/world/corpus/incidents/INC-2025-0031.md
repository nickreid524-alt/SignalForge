## Summary
All payment authorisations failed for 2 hours 40 minutes starting at exactly 00:00 UTC.

## Timeline
- 00:00 error rate went to 100%; logs showed `x509: certificate has expired or is not yet valid`.
- 00:03 `payments-error-rate` SEV1 fired.
- 00:20 on-call initially suspected the previous afternoon's payments release and started a rollback, which changed nothing.
- 01:10 certificate expiry identified from the log line's `not_after` value.
- 02:40 new certificate installed; recovery immediate.

## Root cause
The client certificate issued by PayVault had a twelve-month lifetime and no owner. The 30-day expiry alert was configured as notify-only.

## Contributing factors
- A payments deployment nine hours before onset drew attention away from the exact-midnight onset.

## Remediation
- Certificate inventory created; expiry alert now pages. Procedure captured in RB-016.

## Lessons
- An onset at exactly 00:00 UTC is an expiry until proven otherwise.
