## Summary
Roughly 30% of logins failed for 75 minutes; the failures were confined to two of six gateway nodes.

## Timeline
- 06:30 `login-failure-rate` fired.
- 06:50 gateway logs showed `token rejected: not yet valid (nbf)` on two nodes only.
- 07:20 NTP found stopped on those nodes; restarted.
- 07:45 recovery.

## Root cause
Clock skew on two gateway nodes; tokens with `nbf` set to issuance time were rejected as future-dated.

## Remediation
- NTP health check added to node readiness.

## Lessons
- Read the rejection reason; `unknown kid`, `expired`, `nbf` and `invalid signature` have different causes.
