## Summary
For 65 minutes, about 4% of checkout requests timed out at the edge gateway. The onset matched checkout release v2025.03.12-1 within three minutes.

## Timeline
- 14:02 checkout v2025.03.12-1 finished deploying.
- 14:05 `checkout-p99-slo` alert fired.
- 14:40 rollback started after change notes were reviewed.
- 15:10 metrics back to baseline.

## Root cause
The release changed the payments client to retry three times with a six-second timeout. Under normal PayVault latency this was harmless, but any slow authorisation stacked retries beyond the 15 s gateway timeout.

## Contributing factors
- Change notes described the retry change as a resilience improvement without mentioning the latency budget.

## Remediation
- Rolled back; retry budget capped at the gateway timeout in a later release.

## Lessons
- Retry and timeout changes need a latency-budget review.
