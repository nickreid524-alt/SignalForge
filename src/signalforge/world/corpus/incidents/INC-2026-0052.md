## Summary
A low rate of 502 responses persisted for a day and a half. No service, deployment or configuration change explained it.

## Timeline
- 04:00 `gateway-5xx-ratio-low` fired.
- Day 1: every upstream service was investigated; each showed healthy metrics. Gateway logs showed `connection reset by peer` skewed towards upstreams in zone az-b.
- Day 2 10:00 zone az-b drained as an experiment; 502s stopped.
- 16:00 node NIC firmware bug confirmed by the platform provider.

## Root cause
Packet loss on zone az-b nodes caused by a NIC firmware defect. Application-level metrics could not show it; only the zone skew in gateway connection errors hinted at it.

## Remediation
- Node network-quality telemetry added to the platform dashboards.

## Lessons
- When application telemetry is exhausted, say so. The honest conclusion was insufficient evidence with a zone skew suggesting infrastructure, long before the drain experiment.
