## Summary
Orders returned 503 for 8-10% of requests once its connection pool reached the maximum of 50.

## Timeline
- 08:50 orders v2025.04.03-1 deployed.
- 10:20 `orders-5xx-rate` fired; logs showed `timeout acquiring connection from pool`.
- 11:30 the leak was found in the validation error path; rollback started.
- 12:00 pool recovered.

## Root cause
A refactor returned early on validation errors without releasing the borrowed connection. Each invalid order leaked one connection until the pool was exhausted.

## Contributing factors
- `db_pool_active` climbed for ninety minutes before alerting; no gradient alert existed.
- orders-db `connections_by_client` showed only the orders client, which correctly excluded other clients.

## Remediation
- Rolled back; added a pool-utilisation gradient alert.

## Lessons
- A slow climb after a release points at the service; a step to the maximum points at a competing client.
