title: Disk pressure on batch nodes

Batch nodes (reporting, warehouse loaders) write large logs and temporary files. Disk pressure shows as `disk_usage` above 90% and write errors.

## Diagnosis
1. Query `disk_usage` over seven days. Identify the moment the slope changed; correlate with configuration changes on the node.
2. Look for `logrotate.enabled`, `log_retention_days` or `tmp.cleanup` keys set to disabled values.
3. Read the ERROR logs for `No space left on device` to confirm.

## Remediation
- Restore rotation, compress or delete rotated logs, and rerun the failed job.
- If the growth is data rather than logs, expand the volume and open a capacity ticket.
