Payments authorises cards through PayVault over mutual TLS. Nearly every payments incident is either PayVault, the network path to PayVault, or credentials for PayVault.

## Symptoms
- `payments-error-rate` alert; checkout completions failing at the payment step.

## Diagnosis
1. Read the payments ERROR logs and classify the failure: `timeout` (processor slow), `handshake` or `x509` (certificate or TLS), `declined` (business, not an outage).
2. Check `get_service_health` for the `payvault` node: its status feed says whether PayVault itself reports an incident.
3. Query `dependency_latency_p95` with `dependency=payvault` on payments.
4. Check the onset time. A failure that starts at exactly midnight UTC is almost always an expiry (certificate, key or token), not a deployment.
5. Only then review payments deployments and configuration changes; a timeout increase applied after onset is a mitigation attempt, not a cause.

## Remediation
- Certificate expired: RB-016.
- PayVault outage: RB-018 (fallback processor).
