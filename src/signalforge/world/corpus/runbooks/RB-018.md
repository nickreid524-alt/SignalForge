When PayVault has a regional outage, payments can route authorisations to the secondary processor. Fallback is a business decision because fees differ; the on-call may enable it for SEV1 incidents.

## When to use
- PayVault's status feed reports a partial or major outage in our region.
- `dependency_latency_p95` for payvault is far above normal with timeouts, not declines.
- No internal change explains the onset. Raising `payvault.timeout_ms` during the incident is a mitigation and does not change the cause.

## Procedure
1. Set `processor.fallback_enabled=true` on payments through the config tool.
2. Watch `error_rate` fall within two minutes.
3. Disable fallback once PayVault reports resolved and our timeouts stop.
