A guide to the payments dashboards for on-call engineers.

## Panels
- **Authorisations**: `request_rate` split by outcome; declines are normal, timeouts are not.
- **Processor latency**: `dependency_latency_p95` with `dependency=payvault`.
- **Error ratio**: `error_rate`; anything above 2% pages.
- **Pool**: `db_pool_active` against `db_pool_max` for payments-db.

## Operator tips
- Compare with the same hour yesterday before declaring an anomaly.
- The status feed for the `payvault` node is fetched every minute; a `major_outage` state is authoritative for their side.
