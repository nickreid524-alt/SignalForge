title: Database connection pool exhaustion
service: platform

Each service has a fixed connection pool per database. Exhaustion shows as `db_pool_active` pinned at `db_pool_max` with acquisition timeouts in the logs.

## Two different causes
- **The service leaks or holds connections**: `db_pool_active` climbs slowly after a release; the database sees the same client holding many idle-in-transaction sessions.
- **Another client starves the database**: the database's `connections_by_client` shows a client that normally uses the replica (reporting, analytics) suddenly on the primary, or a client with a much larger footprint than before. The primary reaches its own limit and every service pool stalls.

## Diagnosis
1. On the database node, query `connections_by_client` for all clients over the last two hours.
2. On the affected service, compare `db_pool_active` to `db_pool_max`.
3. Check configuration changes on every client of the database, not only the failing service. Look for `db_host`, `replica`, `primary` or `pool_size` keys.
4. Check `cpu_utilization` on the database; long analytical queries raise it sharply.

## Remediation
- Starving client: point it back at the replica, kill its long-running queries.
- Leak: roll back the service release; raise the pool size only as a temporary measure.
