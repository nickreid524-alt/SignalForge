Use this checklist before attributing an incident to a deployment.

## Checklist
1. **Onset alignment**: the symptom starts within minutes after the deployment finished. A deployment hours earlier that was stable is weak evidence.
2. **Mechanism**: the change notes describe something that can plausibly produce the symptom (fan-out, retries, defaults, schema).
3. **Locality**: the deployment is on the failing service or on a service the failing service depends on, including producers of events the failing service consumes.
4. **Exclusion**: no configuration change or external status change explains the symptom better.
5. **Confirmation**: a metric or log pattern changes at the deployment time, not just an alert.

## Anti-patterns
- Blaming the most recent deployment anywhere in the platform.
- Ignoring configuration changes because they are not releases.
- Treating a mitigation applied after onset as the cause.
