The fulfillment worker consumes `order.placed` from the event bus and creates shipments. Backlog means orders stay in `placed`.

## Symptoms
- `fulfillment-consumer-lag` alert; `queue_lag` and `queue_depth` rising.
- Orders visible in the UI but no shipments.

## Distinguish three shapes
- **Too few consumers**: `replica_count` below the normal six, `restart_count` zero, each consumer healthy. Check autoscaler configuration changes (`min_replicas`, `max_replicas`).
- **Consumers crashing**: `restart_count` high, error logs with stack traces, event-bus `redelivery_count` rising. See RB-008 and the producer's recent deployments; a schema change upstream often breaks the consumer.
- **Bus problem**: event-bus health degraded, leader elections, partitions offline. A completed leader election that recovered within a minute does not explain a backlog that starts hours later.

## Remediation
- Scale the worker back up or revert the autoscaler change.
- Crash loop: fix or roll back the offending change (usually upstream), then let the backlog drain.
