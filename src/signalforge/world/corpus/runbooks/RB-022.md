Autoscaler policies (`min_replicas`, `max_replicas`, `target_cpu`) are configuration, not code, and are changed through the config tool with a ticket.

## Risks
- Lowering `min_replicas` on a queue consumer reduces throughput immediately; lag appears within minutes and grows linearly.
- Raising `target_cpu` delays scale-out under bursts.
- Consumers scale on CPU, and a starved consumer is not CPU-bound, so the autoscaler will not scale it back up on its own.

## Diagnosis pattern
- `replica_count` drops at the minute of the configuration change while `restart_count` stays at zero and each replica reports healthy.

## Remediation
- Revert the policy; confirm `replica_count` recovers and lag drains.
