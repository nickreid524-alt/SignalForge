Payments authenticates to PayVault with a client certificate issued by PayVault for twelve months. When it expires, every TLS handshake fails at once.

## Symptoms
- 100% payments error rate starting at exactly the certificate's `not_after` time, typically 00:00 UTC.
- Logs: `x509: certificate has expired or is not yet valid`.
- PayVault's status feed shows operational.

## Rotation procedure
1. Generate a new key pair and CSR in the payments secret store.
2. Submit the CSR through the PayVault merchant console; download the signed certificate.
3. Store the certificate; restart payments to pick it up.
4. Confirm `error_rate` returns to baseline and handshake errors stop.

## Prevention
- The `certificate-expiry-30d` alert must page, not just notify. Check the certificate inventory monthly.
