Reporting runs a nightly ETL from the orders-db replica into the warehouse and renders management reports at 02:00 UTC.

## Symptoms
- Reports missing in the morning; ETL stages failing in the logs.
- `reporting-disk-usage` alert.

## Diagnosis
1. Read the last ETL errors in the logs. `No space left on device` is a disk problem, not a data problem.
2. If disk: query `disk_usage` over the last seven days. A steady linear climb points at log or temp-file retention; a step points at a single large file.
3. Check configuration changes on reporting for `logrotate`, `retention` or `tmp` keys.
4. Consumer-lag alerts for `reporting-etl` on the event bus are a symptom of the job not running, not a cause.

## Remediation
- Re-enable rotation or retention, delete rotated logs, rerun the job.
- See RB-011 for general disk pressure handling.
